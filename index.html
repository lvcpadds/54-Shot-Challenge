54-Shot Challenge

Full routine, real target, every shot. Tap a box once you've hit it \u2014 rate yourself 1\u20135 in your head as you go (5 = exactly what you meant to do).

1 Five different clubs, five different targets

One shot per club. New target every time. Tap a box to log a shot (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust or clear.

2 One club, five shot shapes

Pick a club below, then hit a high, a low, a fade (curving right), a draw (curving left), and a half shot with it. Tap a box to log a shot (defaults to a 4), tap again to bump the rating or cycle back to clear.

3 Opposite-handed, five shots

Turn a club around \u2014 righty swings lefty, or lefty swings righty. Tap to log (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust.

4 Match the carry, five shots

Full 8-iron first, note the carry. Then hit 7i, 6i, 5i, fairway wood, and driver that all carry that same distance. Tap to log (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust.

5 Break your stance, three shots

7-iron with feet together \u2192 7-iron standing on your left leg \u2192 7-iron with eyes closed. Tap to log (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust.

6 Out of a divot, five shots

Five different clubs, all from a divot hole. Tap to log (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust.

7 Five clubs, five targets \u2014 again

Close it out same as you started: five clubs, five fresh targets. Tap to log (defaults to a 4), tap again to adjust.
